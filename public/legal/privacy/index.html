Bank connectivity is provided by licensed AIS partners (for example Enable Banking) and the account holder’s bank under PSD2/open-banking consent. Hosting of this static notice is on Vercel (this site). No advertising trackers are used on this page.

Transaction caches and secrets remain under the owner’s control and can be deleted at any time. Bank consent can be revoked via the bank and/or the AIS provider.
